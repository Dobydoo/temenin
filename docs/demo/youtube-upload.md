## Title

Temenin — Find a Verified Activity Buddy (Hackathon Demo)

## Description

In a city of millions, the hardest part of the weekend isn't picking
a movie. It's finding someone to go with.

Temenin matches you with a verified activity buddy for the plan you
already have. Post the plan, get ranked candidates with explainable
scores, and meet safely: names, places, and times stay sealed until
both sides are verified, and the exact meeting point opens only after
a two-sided confirmation.

Vibes click. Plans stick.

HOW IT WORKS
- Deterministic matching from 5 components: gender preference (30%),
  shared interests (25%), energy fit (20%), context (15%), trust (10%).
- Privacy enforced in the data layer, not just hidden on screen.
- Chat holds phone numbers and social handles until the meetup is confirmed.
- Venues come only from a curated public list. The app never invents a place.

Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Indie developer track,
Build for Your World). Next.js + React + TypeScript + Tailwind. All names
are fictional demo personas.
